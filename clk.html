The Mercedes-Benz CLK GTR

The Mercedes-Benz CLK GTR is one of the most legendary supercars ever created. It was designed for racing and became an icon of Mercedes-Benz performance.

About the Car

The CLK GTR was developed in 1997 for the FIA GT Championship. Mercedes-Benz created a road version so the car could meet racing regulations.

Specifications

  • Engine: 6.9L V12
  • Power: Around 612 HP
  • Top Speed: Around 214 MPH
  • Transmission: 6-Speed Manual
  • Production: 1998-1999
  • Type: Supercar
