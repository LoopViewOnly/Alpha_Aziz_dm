The Mercedes-Benz S-Class

Cares for what matters. The luxury sedan redefined.

Luxury Without Compromise

The Mercedes-Benz S-Class stands for the fascination of Mercedes-Benz: legendary and traditional engineering expertise defines the luxury segment in the automotive industry. You can experience the S-Class with all your senses — seeing, feeling, hearing, and smelling — while offering numerous innovations in safety, comfort, and performance.

Key Specifications

Engine

3.0L Inline-6 Turbo with EQ Boost / 4.0L V8 Biturbo

Horsepower

429 hp – 496 hp

0-60 mph

4.4 seconds

Drivetrain

4MATIC® Fully Variable All-Wheel Drive

Innovative Features

  • MBUX Multimedia System: Augmented reality navigation and artificial intelligence that adapts to user habits.
  • E-ACTIVE BODY CONTROL: Fully active suspension that reads the road and adjusts each wheel individually.
  • Rear-Axle Steering: Turn tightly into parking spaces with up to 10-degree rear wheel steering.
  • Burmester® 4D Surround Sound: Sound tailored to every seat using seat-integrated tactile transducers.
