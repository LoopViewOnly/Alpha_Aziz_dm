The Mercedes-Benz G-Class, also known as the G-Wagon, is a luxury SUV made by the German company Mercedes-Benz. It is famous for its boxy design, powerful engine, luxurious interior, and excellent off-road capabilities. 🚘 Basic Information - Manufacturer: Mercedes-Benz, Germany - First introduced: 1979 - Vehicle type: Luxury SUV - Design: Boxy shape with round headlights - Drivetrain: Four-wheel drive (4WD) - Main uses: City driving, travel, and off-road adventures - Popular models: G 500 and G 63 AMG
